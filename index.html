Vietnam Airport Arrival Notes

Collected notes on arriving at Vietnam's three main international airports - what the immigration queues actually look like, how the e-visa flow works, and when paying for a fast-track meet & greet makes sense.

Airport guides

  • Noi Bai (HAN), Hanoi - two predictable queue banks at Terminal 2: the 5-8am European arrivals and the 7-10pm East Asia evening bank.
  • Tan Son Nhat (SGN), Ho Chi Minh City - the longest immigration waits in the country, routinely 60-100 minutes in the evening.
  • Da Nang (DAD) - usually the quickest of the three, but it still spikes during Tet, summer peak, and charter flight banks.
